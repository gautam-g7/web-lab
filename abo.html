Innovate. Develop. Lead the Digital Future.
The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) at Mohandas College of Engineering and Technology (MCET) is a two-year postgraduate programme affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU), Kerala. The programme is designed to develop highly skilled computing professionals capable of addressing the evolving challenges of the global technology ecosystem.
With a strong emphasis on academic excellence, innovation, and industry relevance, the programme provides an advanced understanding of computer science, software engineering, intelligent computing, cloud technologies, data analytics, and secure computing. Students are equipped with the knowledge and practical competencies required to design, develop, deploy, and manage modern software systems and digital solutions.
The curriculum follows a balanced approach that integrates theoretical foundations with hands-on learning through advanced laboratories, software development practices, technical seminars, domain expertise workshops, research activities, and major projects. This holistic learning environment enables students to cultivate technical proficiency, critical thinking, problem-solving ability, professional ethics, and leadership skills, preparing them for successful careers in the IT industry, research organizations, and technology-driven enterprises.

Programme Structure
The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) is a two-year postgraduate programme comprising four semesters, structured according to the academic regulations of APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU).

The programme consists of:
  • Core Courses
  • Professional Elective Courses
  • Laboratory Courses
  • Mini Project
  • Seminar
  • Domain Expertise Workshops
  • Comprehensive Viva
  • Major Project


The curriculum emphasizes practical learning through extensive laboratory sessions, project work, and industry-oriented training, enabling students to gain the skills required for successful careers in the IT industry.

Programme Duration:
2 Years (4 Semesters)
